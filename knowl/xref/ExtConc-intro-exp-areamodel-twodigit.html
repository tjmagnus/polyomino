Exercise 11. Area Model for Two-digit Numbers.

In Exercise 1.1.5.9, you showed how to use an area model to represent multiplication of numbers. The role of place value in the multiplication of two-digit numbers can also be represented using the area model: for example, to multiply 47 by 25 we can represent 47 as 4 tens and 7 ones and 25 as 2 tens and 5 ones. We then can sketch the product as shown in Figure 1.1.9.
